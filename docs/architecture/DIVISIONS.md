A **division** is a top-level capability domain. It owns its data and is the
sole writer of it; other divisions read through its service interface rather
than writing its tables (Constitution P7, §8).

Division numbers are permanent. A division is never renumbered, and a retired
division's number is never reissued (Constitution P6).
